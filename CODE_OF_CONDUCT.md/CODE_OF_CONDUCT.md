# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone. We commit to creating an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include showing empathy, respecting different opinions, accepting constructive feedback, and taking responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language, and sharing private information without permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and taking fair corrective action. They may remove contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces and whenever someone officially represents the community in public.

## Enforcement

Report abusive or unacceptable behavior to the community leaders responsible for enforcement. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and reporter privacy will be respected.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will apply proportionate consequences based on the nature and impact of the behavior. These may include a correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban.
